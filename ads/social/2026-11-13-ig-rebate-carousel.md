## 캡션 A (설명형)
A futures fee rebate is a share of the fees you already pay, sent back to you after you trade. Same exchange, same order book, same listed rate.

On a $10,000 trade, in and out as a taker at Bitget's standard 0.06%, you pay $12.00. With 40% back, $4.80 returns in USDT and your effective rate is 0.036%.

Funding is not covered, and leverage risk stays the same. Full walkthrough: link in bio.
