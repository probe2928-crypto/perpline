BTCC referral FAQ

What is the BTCC referral code?

Our BTCC referral code is DDHOR6. Register through our link, then send your UID to @btcbullit on Telegram to activate the 50% fee rebate.

How much rebate do I get on BTCC?

Perpline returns 50% of the futures trading fees you pay on BTCC, paid weekly in USDT. On the standard taker fee of 0.045%, that brings your effective rate to 0.022%.

What are BTCC futures fees?

The standard (non-VIP) USDT-margined perpetual fee on BTCC is 0.025% maker and 0.045% taker, checked Oct 2026. Your live account rate applies.

Can I add a BTCC referral code to an existing account?

Usually not. Exchanges link a referral when the account is created, so the rebate requires a new BTCC account opened through our link.

Do you need my password or API key?

No. We only need the exchange name and your account UID. Never share your password, 2FA codes or API keys with anyone.

Is BTCC available in my country?

BTCC decides which countries it serves. Check its terms for your country before registering. This site is not for residents of the Republic of Korea or the United States.
